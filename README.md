# Exsilium
*NeoForge 1.21.1 | Adventure-Survival Overhaul*

## You were never meant to survive here.

**Exsilium** drops you into a world that has moved on. The terrain is alien, the ruins are deep, and the dragons never stopped growing. Built for explorers, survivalists, and anyone who thinks vanilla Minecraft was just a little too forgiving.

---

### What Awaits

**🌍 A World Remade**
Terralith, Tectonic, and Biomes O' Plenty rewrite the surface. YUNG's complete suite rebuilds every dungeon, temple, mineshaft, and stronghold. The End and Nether have been rebuilt from bedrock to ceiling. If you think you've seen it all, you haven't.

**🐉 Mythic Monsters & Ancient Ruins**
Ice and Fire dragons blot out the sun. Alex's Mobs and Mowzie's Mobs fill the quiet biomes with things that bite. L_Ender's Cataclysm waits in the dark for anyone foolish enough to dig straight down. With Enhanced AI, even the familiar faces hit harder.

**❄️ Survival of the Exile**
Tough As Nails and Serene Seasons turn the weather into a weapon. Freeze in the mountains. Overheat in the deserts. Thirst is real, and when you die, the Gravestone Mod keeps your gear—if you can make it back alive.

**⚔️ Loot, Economy, & Wayfaring**
Artifacts grant legendary abilities. Lightman's Currency builds a real economy. Waystones let you carve safe paths through the madness. Small Ships turn rivers into highways. Xaero's maps record a world too big to memorize.

**🎧 Immersion Without Compromise**
See for miles with Distant Horizons. Run shader-ready with Iris + Sodium. AmbientSounds, Sound Physics Remastered, Wakes, Particular, and Visuality make the world feel physical. A rainstorm is a warning. A midnight forest is a threat.

**🛠️ Complete Crafting Insight**
Full EMI suite (EMI, EMI Loot, EMI Ores, EMI Enchanting, EMIffect, Extra Mod Integrations) means you always know what is possible, what drops where, and how to survive another night.

**🎤 Built for Multiplayer**
Simple Voice Chat and Ping Wheel make group exiles seamless. Form a caravan, crew a ship, or split up to map the world. Just don't wander alone after dark.

---

### Performance & Setup
- **Modloader:** NeoForge 1.21.1
- **Renderer:** Sodium + Iris (shader compatible, Distant Horizons ready)
- **Optimization:** ModernFix, Ferrite Core, Lithium, ImmediatelyFast, EntityCulling, CullLeaves, and more
- **Recommended RAM:** 6–8 GB
