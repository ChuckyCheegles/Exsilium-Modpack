# Exsilium
*NeoForge 1.21.1 | Adventure-Survival Overhaul*

## You were never meant to survive here.

**Exsilium** drops you into a world that has moved on. The terrain is alien, the ruins are deep, and the dragons never stopped growing. Built for explorers, survivalists, and anyone who thinks vanilla Minecraft was just a little too forgiving.

---

## What Awaits

### 🌍 A World Remade
Terralith, Tectonic, and Biomes O' Plenty rewrite the surface. YUNG's complete suite rebuilds every dungeon, temple, mineshaft, and stronghold. Dynamic Trees makes forests truly towering. Luki's structure mods and The Roads More Travelled ensure every journey has a discovery. If you think you've seen it all, you haven't.

### 🐉 Mythic Monsters & Ancient Ruins
Alex's Mobs Continued and creature mods like Hybrid Aquatic and Hybrid Birds fill the quiet biomes with things that bite. Adorable Hamster Pets offer a brief moment of peace—before the monsters find you.

### ❄️ Survival of the Exile
Tough As Nails and Serene Seasons turn the weather into a weapon. Freeze in the mountains. Overheat in the deserts. When you die, the Yet Another Gravestone Mod keeps your gear—if you can make it back alive.

### ⚔️ Loot, Economy, & Wayfaring
Xaero's Minimap and World Map record a world too big to memorize. Traveler's Titles mark your conquests. RoadArchitect lets you build highways through the madness.

### 🎧 Immersion Without Compromise
See for miles with Distant Horizons. Run shader-ready with Iris + Sodium. AmbientSounds, Sound Physics Remastered, Wakes, Particular Reforged, and Visuality: Reforged make the world feel physical. A rainstorm is a warning. A midnight forest is a threat.

### 🛠️ Complete Crafting Insight
EMI means you always know what is possible, what drops where, and how to survive another night.

### 🎤 Built for Multiplayer
Simple Voice Chat makes group exiles seamless. Form a caravan or split up to map the world. Just don't wander alone after dark.

---

## Installation

1. **[Click here](https://chuckycheegles.github.io/Exsilium-Modpack/)** to download the modpack.
2. The `exsilium.mrpack` file will start downloading automatically.
3. Double-click the downloaded file to open it in the Modrinth App.  
   *If nothing happens, open the Modrinth App → **Create Instance** → **Import from file** → select the downloaded file.*
4. In the Modrinth App, select **Exsilium** and click **Play**.

---

## Performance & Setup

- **Modloader:** NeoForge 1.21.1 [1]
- **Renderer:** Sodium + Iris (shader compatible, Distant Horizons ready) [1]
- **Optimization:** ModernFix, Ferrite Core, Lithium, ImmediatelyFast, EntityCulling, and more [1]
- **Recommended RAM:** 6–8 GB [1]
